ABOUT THE GAME

More than chess.
It's a battle of minds.

Chess Arena Combat is a strategic mobile chess game developed by WASFORGE STUDIOS.

Chess Arena Combat was created to give chess a more immersive and modern presentation while preserving the strategic depth of the game. It combines classic chess gameplay with a modern cinematic interface, AI competition and a professional dark arena atmosphere.

The experience is built around strategy, decision-making, tactical thinking and improving through gameplay — using the traditional chess pieces and rules players already know, presented inside a competitive arena setting.

FAQ

Common questions.

What is Chess Arena Combat?

Chess Arena Combat is a mobile chess game developed by WASFORGE STUDIOS. It combines traditional chess rules and pieces with a modern, cinematic game interface.

Who developed Chess Arena Combat?

Chess Arena Combat is developed by WASFORGE STUDIOS, an independent game studio.

Is Chess Arena Combat a chess game?

Yes. Chess Arena Combat uses the standard rules and pieces of classic chess: King, Queen, Rook, Bishop, Knight and Pawn.

Does Chess Arena Combat have AI?

Yes. Players can challenge a built-in AI opponent at Easy, Medium or Hard difficulty.

What AI difficulty levels are available?

Chess Arena Combat offers three AI difficulty levels: Easy, Medium and Hard.

Can players choose White or Black?

Yes. Before each game, players can choose to play as White, Black, or have their side assigned at Random.

What platform is Chess Arena Combat available on?

Chess Arena Combat is built for Android.
